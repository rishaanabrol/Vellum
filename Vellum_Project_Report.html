1. Problem Statement

Modern students and researchers increasingly rely on Generative AI and Large Language Models (LLMs) to synthesize dense academic course materials, problem sets, and textbooks. However, general-purpose LLM chat interfaces present three critical limitations in academic settings:

  • Hallucination & Lack of Verifiability: AI models routinely produce plausible-sounding yet factually inaccurate statements without providing auditable, page-level citations or mathematical proof.
  • The Speculation Trap (No Refusal Cutoff): When course materials do not contain the answer, conventional chatbots speculate using general pre-training data rather than admitting the answer is absent from the syllabus, misleading students.
  • Context Loss & Prompt Injection Vulnerability: General interfaces fail to isolate user-uploaded documents from core instructions, risking syllabus confusion or adversarial prompt injection.

2. Why This Problem Statement Was Chosen

Academic study demands strict intellectual accountability: "Don't just answer the student; show them exactly where the answer came from."

"When university students prepare for examinations or technical coursework, ungrounded speculation is worse than no answer. A student needs deterministic proof, exact equation derivations, and the ability to verify original pages with confidence."
  • Pedagogical Rigor: In STEM courses (physics, linear algebra, engineering), answers must cite exact formula derivations and test parameters rather than hand-wavy approximations.
  • Verifiable Evidence: Combining conversational intelligence with high-resolution PDF page rasterization allows immediate confirmation of underlying diagrams, charts, and equations.
  • Editorial Focus: Designed with an academic, print-monograph reading aesthetic that promotes focused, distraction-free scholarship.

3. Approach / Proposed Solution

Vellum implements an end-to-end, deterministic Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipeline specifically engineered around page provenance, high-resolution rendering, and strict citation verification:

3. Architectural Highlights (Continued)

  • Page-Aware Ingestion: PyMuPDF (fitz) extracts document text page-by-page while tracking character density to flag scanned or low-quality pages.
  • Paragraph-Preserving Chunking: Content is segmented along natural semantic paragraphs (\n\n) with controlled overlap, ensuring mathematical formulas and proofs stay intact.
  • Dual-Stream Hybrid Retrieval: Combines dense semantic vector search (gemini-embedding-001) with lexical keyword matching, capturing abstract concepts while preserving exact variable names and theorem definitions.
  • Deterministic Refusal Cutoff: If retrieved chunks score below relevance threshold (MIN_RELEVANCE = 0.65), the LLM generation step is bypassed entirely to prevent hallucination.
  • Automated Citation Validation: Regex post-processors parse bracketed citations ([1], [2]), verifying every citation maps to retrieved evidence chunks and stripping hallucinated markers.
  • Page Evidence Proof: Original PDF pages are rendered on demand in high resolution within the Evidence panel, providing visual verification of charts, proofs, and figures.

5. Key Features & Functionality

Continuous 3-Column Desk

Organized desktop keeping document library, conversation timeline, and evidence citations simultaneously accessible without context switching.

Automated Document Insights

Generates high-yield summaries and 3 clickable starter study questions tailored to uploaded problem sets, lecture slides, or syllabi.

Context-Aware Query Rewriting

Follow-up queries (e.g. "Explain that step in simpler terms") are automatically rewritten into self-contained standalone search queries using past context.

Visual Proof & Page Render

The Evidence panel renders the exact rasterized PDF page image, letting students verify original layout, diagrams, and equations visually.

Step-by-Step Solutions

Formulates math problems (matrices, eigenvalues, wave equations) with complete step-by-step LaTeX solutions and verified bracketed citations [1], [2].

Notes Export & Cloud Ready

One-click export of chat sessions into formatted Markdown study notes. Built-in support for Streamlit Cloud deployment with encrypted secrets management.
